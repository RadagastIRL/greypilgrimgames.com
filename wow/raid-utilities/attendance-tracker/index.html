Raid Attendance

Connects to WarcraftLogs to pull your guild's attendance automatically — no manual entry ever.

1 WarcraftLogs API Client
Go to warcraftlogs.com/api/clients and click Create Client. Name it anything (e.g. "Attendance Tracker"), set the redirect URI to https://localhost, then copy the Client ID and Secret below. Your credentials are stored only in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere except WarcraftLogs.
2 Guild Info
Used to find your guild's logs on WarcraftLogs and pull your roster from Raider.IO to automatically tell guild members from pugs.
