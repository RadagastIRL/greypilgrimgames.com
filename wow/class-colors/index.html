How to play
⭐ Recommended: Timed Mode — 30 seconds

Race the clock and answer as many questions as you can before time runs out. The timer turns orange at 15s and red at 10s. Questions auto-advance so you can keep your rhythm. When time's up, a results card shows your score — and you can share it Wordle-style (see Sharing below).

Game modes

Timed — 30 seconds, auto-advancing questions, shareable results. Select it in the bar above.

Zen Mode — no clock, unlimited questions, answer at your own pace. Your correct, wrong, and streak counts carry across the session.

Playstyles

Color → Class — a color swatch appears. Type the name of the WoW class that uses it and press Enter or Submit.

Class → Color — a class name appears. Click the matching color swatch in the grid.

Mixed — both question types are shuffled together at random.

Typing answers

Only letters and spaces are accepted. If your input isn't a class name, you get a warning and another try — after 3 invalid entries in a row the question is marked wrong.

The Hint button reveals the first letter and character count. Skip reveals the answer and counts as wrong. In Zen Mode mode, press Space or click Next to advance.

Scoring

Streak counts consecutive correct answers and resets on any wrong answer or skip. Reset Score clears everything and reshuffles the deck.

📊 Share your score

After a Timed round, hit Share Results to copy a Wordle-style scorecard to your clipboard — each question is a ✅ or ❌ in rows of five. Paste it anywhere to show off (or commiserate).
