Initiative Tracker
Dual-view tracker with a public player screen and a private DM view. The DM can keep enemies hidden until revealed; both views stay in sync in real time — works at the table or shared over a video call.

Dice Roller
True-random rolls for any die — d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100 — with support for multi-dice expressions (e.g. 3d6+2) and a roll history log.

Build and save full character sheets in the browser. Export to JSON for re-import later, or generate a print-ready PDF to bring to the table.
